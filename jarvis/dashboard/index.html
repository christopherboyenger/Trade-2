            Methodology. Charts and replay use public 1h candles fetched by your browser — Binance (500 bars), falling back to Coinbase (300) or Kraken when a provider is unreachable or geo-blocked; the banner names the active source. The replay mirrors the bot's exact rule set and cost model (fills at close ±5bps slippage, 10bps fee; stops/trails checked bar-by-bar) — it is a faithful mirror, not the bot itself. The authoritative system, with its SQLite ledger, circuit breakers and kill switch, runs in the Clodds gateway (see PR #8).

            This is paper money. Nothing here places real orders. Promotion to live capital requires all six gates green (≥30 trades, ≥60 days, PF>1.3, DD<15%) and then a human decision.
